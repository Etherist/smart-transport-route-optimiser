About This Tool

The Smart Route Optimizer is designed specifically for Australian transport companies to:

  • Reduce fuel costs by 10–20% through optimized routing
  • Ensure compliance with NHVR fatigue management rules and Chain of Responsibility laws
  • Minimize CO₂ emissions for sustainability reporting
  • Improve delivery times by avoiding traffic and weather delays
